🌟 Our Vision

To be the "Best" cat cafe in the Philippines offering the best coffee in town while spreading its advocacy for the homeless cats.

Established as Nueva Ecija's first community cat cafe, Hello Meow Cafe is more than just a cozy spot for coffee and pastries. Our core mission is deeply rooted in animal welfare, providing proper nourishment, medical care, and safe social spaces for rescue felines.

🐾 Our Mission

EAT: Serving quality and fresh food.

DRINK: Offering the best coffee in town.

SUPPORT: Spreading love to the voiceless animals.

PET: Helping people mentally, and emotionally lower stress through kitty therapy.

MEOW: Spreading meowy good vibes.

The Cat Lounge

Unwind in our cozy, safe haven designed for both human relaxation and feline comfort. Interact with friendly rescues, read a book, or enjoy your coffee surrounded by purrs.

📜

Sanctuary Rules

We prioritize the health, safety, and peace of our rescue cats.

  • ✓ Sanitize hands before entering the lounge area.
  • ✓ Do not wake sleeping cats or pull tails/paws.
  • ✓ Take photos without flash to protect sensitive eyes.
  • ✓ Only feed cats approved treats provided by staff.
Respect our feline hosts & have fun! 🐾
