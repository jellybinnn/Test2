A Sanctuary Built on Compassion & Community

Established as Nueva Ecija's first community cat cafe, Hello Meow Cafe is more than just a cozy spot for coffee and pastries. Our core mission is deeply rooted in animal welfare, providing proper nourishment, medical care, and safe social spaces for rescue felines.

We advocate for responsible pet care and animal well-being while matching visitors with a heartwarming, therapeutic environment to relax and unwind alongside our feline residents.

Sanctuary Rules

We prioritize the health, safety, and peace of our rescue cats.

  • ✓ Sanitize hands before entering the lounge area.
  • ✓ Do not wake sleeping cats or pull tails/paws.
  • ✓ Take photos without flash to protect sensitive eyes.
  • ✓ Only feed cats approved treats provided by staff.
Respect our feline hosts & have fun! 🐾
